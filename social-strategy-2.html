Social Strategy No. 2 · Conversations

One conversation.
Four things to share.

We record a conversation with someone behind Batavia's public art, about anything worth hearing. Every recording becomes an interactive article, three short social videos, and an email. No shoot days, no content calendar to feed, no on-camera nerves.

Why it's simpler

  • One 30–45 minute recording a month. That's the whole ask.
  • Guests talk; they don't perform. Audio is forgiving where video isn't.
  • Guests send a handful of photos or phone videos they already have.
  • Everything else is production, done to a repeatable recipe.

Compare: The Story Engine, the capture-day video plan.

Every conversation ships

The same four pieces, every time. Nothing is optional.

01

Interactive article

A page for the conversation: chapter-by-chapter audio player, written story, pull quotes, and the guest's images placed beside the moments they belong to.

02

Three social clips

30–60 second vertical videos for Reels and Facebook: waveform, captions, a guest image or two. Each one points to the article to hear the rest.

03

Email newsletter

A designed email about the conversation, built, tested and sent from our own email system to the WSS list.

04

Download kit

Clips, captions and a share image in one place, so the guest, committee members and partners can post them too.

The recipe

From a recording to a finished episode.

  1. 01

    Record

    A relaxed 30–45 minute conversation, in person or remote (Riverside or Zoom, each voice on its own track). Any subject: how a mural got made, why a sponsor gives, what a volunteer learned on paint day. A committee member hosts from a one-page question sheet.

  2. 02

    Collect the guest's images

    The guest sends 5–15 photos or short phone videos: sketches, work in progress, install day, the finished piece. Plus a headshot and permission to use them.

  3. 03

    Clean the audio in Descript

    Filler words and false starts removed, long pauses tightened, Studio Sound applied, and levels matched so every episode plays at the same volume (about −16 LUFS). Anything sensitive a guest says is flagged for their approval rather than published by default.

  4. 04

    Chapter it

    The finished audio is split into 5–8 titled chapters timed to the edited recording, so listeners can jump straight to the part they care about.

  5. 05

    Name and place the images

    Every image is renamed to say which conversation and moment it belongs to, hosted on Cloudinary, and placed beside that chapter in the article.

    wss-conv-01-marty-gniech-03-install-day.jpg

    Conversation number · guest · chapter number · moment

  6. 06

    Build the article, clips and email

    The article page, three clips and the email are produced from the same transcript, so names, quotes and links match everywhere. The guest and the committee get one review link before anything goes out.

Email newsletter

Every conversation is also an email.

Written from the same transcript, and designed, tested and sent from our own email system rather than a third-party newsletter service.

  • Designed in the system

    Paste notes, a transcript or a document and the issue is designed in the WSS look. Ask for changes in plain English, undo a revision, preview exactly what readers will get.

  • Tested, then sent

    A test goes to up to five people first, and the list send is refused until that exact email has been tested. Delivery runs through Resend, with an unsubscribe link in every email.

  • The list stays ours

    Website sign-ups flow straight into the contact list. No per-subscriber fees, and anyone who unsubscribes stays unsubscribed.

Who does what

One conversation a month. About an hour of committee time.

Committee

  • Choose and invite the guest
  • Host the conversation from the question sheet
  • Collect the guest's images and permission
  • Approve the review link and send the email

Production

  • Audio cleanup, chapters and transcript
  • Image naming and hosting
  • Article page, three clips and the email
  • Download kit for guests and partners

Turnaround

About one week from recording to review link. Clips post over the following two weeks, each pointing back to the article.
