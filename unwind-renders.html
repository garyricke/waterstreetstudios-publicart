Chosen shot · architectural drawing + home page loop

The planted shot redrawn as an architect's pen-and-wash drawing, traced to line up with the photo. The read

OpenAI for accuracy. Gemini for mood.

OpenAI · picked for 4 of 5

More faithful: straightens the lens distortion, keeps signage legible, and makes changes you'd believe a photographer made. Best for the website, press and anything documenting the real site.

Gemini · picked for 1 of 5

Bolder skies and light, especially from the air, but it garbles any text in the frame and often keeps the fisheye warp. Best for social posts where mood beats precision.

Before anything is published

These are AI-enhanced, not straight photographs, and the planted versions show trees and plants that aren't there yet. Label them that way if used publicly, and plan a shoot with the unWIND benches in frame so the hero images show the actual artwork.
